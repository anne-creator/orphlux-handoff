Data Services for Physical AI

VARIOUS PARADIGMS · SCALE TO GROUND TRUTH

Embodied data has no internet-scale corpus to scrape — it has to be manufactured, one interaction at a time, through the right collection paradigms and rigorous quality control.

B.1 / PROBLEM

Language models can learn from internet text, and vision models can learn from vast amounts of images; but robots lack a ready-made "physical world interaction database" that can be directly scraped. Thus, embodied AI data must be collected one by one in the real world.
B.2 / COLLECTION PARADIGMS

Various Paradigms, One Physical World

Embodied data collection isn't a single method — it's a combination of different paradigms, each suited to a different collection scenario and transfer requirement.

01

Ego-Centric Capture

First-person data captured via wearable devices/cameras, recording human actions and interactions in real environments — scalable without requiring a physical robot.

02

UMI Gripper Capture

Captures human–object manipulation trajectories via a UMI (Universal Manipulation Interface) gripper — combining human dexterity with a robot-executable action representation.

EGO-CENTRIC UMI-GRIPPER TELEOPERATION

03

Real-Robot Teleoperation

Direct teleoperation of physical robot hardware, capturing action trajectories that transfer straight to deployment — no sim-to-real gap.

CAPABILITY

Supports real-robot teleoperation across multiple robot platforms and gripper types, with task scenarios, robot platforms, operating tasks, and data modalities configurable per project — covering the full execution chain from equipment deployment and operator training, through task design and scaled collection, to data QC and delivery.

04

Dexterous Hand

Fine-manipulation data for multi-fingered dexterous hands — covering grasping, in-hand rotation, and bimanual coordination — filling the coverage gap that gripper-only data leaves in fine-motor scenarios.

MODALITIES

Captures multimodal data spanning finger-joint states, fingertip poses, hand poses, object poses, and vision — extensible to tactile, force/torque, and contact-state data depending on hardware capability, covering tasks from basic grasping through high-difficulty dexterous manipulation.

05

Simulation

Real-world 3D assets for robotics simulation, covering everyday objects, tools, furniture, and environmental objects to provide diverse and realistic assets for simulation environments.

DELIVERY

Supports both direct selection from an existing library of real-world 3D assets and custom 3D digitization of specified objects. Assets can be customized to project requirements for object category, scale, and quality, then delivered in formats ready for integration into simulation platforms.
