01 / DATASET OVERVIEW

MULTIMODAL DATA

SCOPE & PURPOSE

The training task.

  • MULTI-STEP REASONING
  • DOMAIN EXPERTS
  • ADVERSARIAL SAMPLES

Collects problems requiring multi-step reasoning (causal judgment, logic puzzles); experts decompose them into minimal reasoning units, build reasoning-flow diagrams, design adversarial samples to catch logical traps, and produce multiple solution versions.
