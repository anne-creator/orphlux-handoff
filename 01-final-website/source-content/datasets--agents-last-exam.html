03 / DATA PRODUCTION & CONSTRUCTION

METHOD & WORKFLOW

Data production strengths

We have expert-level data production capabilities for long-horizon task scenarios. Our workflow covers task design, sandbox environment construction, hidden references, deterministic scorer development, and quality review.
